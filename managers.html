The Managers

14 men. 14 teams. An entirely disproportionate amount of fantasy football.

Rob

Humorous Name United

A founding member and one of the principal architects of the Super 8s, Rob has been around since the original 2013 WhatsApp conversation in which the idea of a mini-league was first floated.

He has subsequently developed the dual role of competitor and backroom operator, serving as the league's Vice Commissioner and taking on a substantial amount of the work behind the scenes.

Humorous Name United has been Rob's home throughout his Super 8s career, making him one of the competition's longest-serving one-club managers.

Woolly

Woolbrohampton

If Super 8s had a Hall of Fame, Woolly would presumably have already complained that the trophy cabinet wasn't big enough.

A founding-era manager and the most decorated figure in Super 8s history, Woolly's Woolbrohampton produced the competition's historic quadruple.

Woolly is the Commissioner of Super 8s, a position which has given him considerable influence over the running of the competition and an increasing reputation for Infantino-esque behind-the-scenes wheeling and dealing.

Dave

Change Name FC

Dave is another of the competition's long-serving managers and one of its most successful. Change Name FC became a remarkably effective vehicle for collecting silverware.

The origins of Change Name FC are rather less glamorous. Dave's first season began with the team name Black Hair FC, a reference to a disastrous trip to the hairdressers in which his hair was dyed completely black.

The FPL authorities apparently considered the name potentially racist and instructed him to change it. Thus Change Name FC was born.

Paddy

Bielsa's Babes

Paddy is one of the original Super 8s characters and was present in the conversation that created the league in August 2013.

He subsequently became one of the competition's most successful managers, winning H2H titles with Bielsa's Babes and previously with other team identities.

Bielsa's Babes became one of the league's great comeback stories, returning to the competition and immediately establishing themselves as a serious force.

Watson

FC Dangerous

Watson has been around Super 8s long enough to have accumulated both silverware and a highly developed ability to analyse the team he didn't pick.

His major historical achievement came in the Classic League, where FC Dangerous B took the title. He also lifted the Super 8s Cup with the same club.

For Watson, the fantasy football season apparently consists of two competitions: the one taking place on the pitch, and the considerably more detailed one taking place retrospectively in his head.

James Dunne

ChampagneSuperRovers

Dunne is one of the league's established managers and a former Classic League champion.

His Super 8s career has included several memorable club identities, including Dinamo Dunlop, Dunston Czechsin and his current side, ChampagneSuperRovers.

Having now spent several seasons around the top end of the league's managerial establishment, Dunne has developed the particularly useful Super 8s skill of looking like he knows exactly what he's doing, regardless of what his Gameweek points actually say.

Bradshaw

Turf Less

Bradshaw has become one of the defining figures of the modern Super 8s era and is now into his eleventh year in the league.

He is the league's foremost Burnley evangelist, seemingly capable of steering almost any conversation towards Turf Moor regardless of the subject matter.

This, combined with a naturally dour outlook, has earned him a reputation as one of the more miserable figures in the league. That slightly undersells him, however: Bradshaw has a very sharp wit, an impressive appetite for puns and an apparently inexhaustible supply of things to say.

Tom

el Guapo

Tom is one of the central figures of Super 8s and one of its most recognisable characters, even if his trophy cabinet is not exactly threatening to collapse under its own weight.

His defining managerial principles have been documented for posterity: do not select Arsenal players, and do not select decent players.

He also has a notable preference for selecting Spurs players wherever possible, despite the repeated evidence that this may not be a particularly effective strategy.

More importantly, Tom is one of the league's central characters and a much-loved presence within it. He was the sole cause of former coach Charlie Delaney leaving the league. For that he deserves major respect.

Kev

BeLucky Again

Kev has had two separate stints in Super 8s, returning to the competition after an earlier spell and subsequently enjoying some of the most successful years of his managerial career.

His recent return has been particularly productive, with BeLucky Again collecting plenty of silverware and Kev establishing himself as one of the more successful managers of the modern era.

He spends his winnings sensibly on retirement, travelling the world and playing golf in increasingly exotic locations.

Crystal

Seattle Scorchers

Crystal manages Seattle Scorchers, which is perhaps the most geographically honest team name in Super 8s. He actually lives in Seattle.

His managerial record is considerably less impressive.

Crystal is, by almost any objective measure, terrible at Super 8s. Despite this, he continues to turn up year after year, throwing his money away, but displaying a level of loyalty to the competition which is either admirable or a failure to recognise when enough is enough.

Paul Nightingale

Roped In Again

Paul Nightingale manages Roped In Again, a name which feels increasingly appropriate for a manager who has remained involved in Super 8s despite the obvious opportunity to escape.

Paul is perhaps the quietest manager in the competition. While others fill the WhatsApp group with opinions, analysis and complaints, Paul tends to sit silently and observe.

There is a growing suspicion that he is actually studying everything everyone says before spending several hours conducting an entirely disproportionate analysis of his squad for the following Gameweek. And then losing.

Rich Sutton

Richard

Rich currently manages Richard, which is not the most inventive team name in Super 8s history.

His previous managerial identities include FC Sunshiiiiiine and Fish Rowe on Toast, suggesting that the naming department has, at least historically, been considerably more ambitious.

A massive Aston Villa fan, Rich brings the appropriate level of optimism and suffering that supporting Villa requires. He is also another member of the league with a fondness for a good pun.

Foz

Atletico Waspo

Foz's Atletico Waspo established themselves as one of the dominant H2H sides of the Super 8s era.

He won consecutive H2H titles and also added a Classic League championship, making him one of the league's genuine serial winners.

Away from the trophies, Foz has also been one of the more prolific contributors to the league's increasingly unnecessary WhatsApp infrastructure.

Rami

Prince Majid Rd FC

Rami is the new boy in Super 8s, joining the competition for the 2026/27 season and taking over at Prince Majid Rd FC.

He now lives in Saudi Arabia, which has immediately created a number of expectations around his financial circumstances.

Rami works in the world of fancy airline lounges in airports, so he is already considerably more glamorous than the average Super 8s manager.

For now, his principal achievement is simply making it into the league. He is delighted to be competing in the top fantasy league in English football.
